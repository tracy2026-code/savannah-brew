Our story

More than a café. A place to belong.

Savannah Brew began with a simple belief: a good café should feel like a second home. We wanted to create a warm space where Nairobi could gather over beautifully brewed Kenyan coffee and food prepared with care.

Our beans are sourced from coffee-growing communities across Kenya, and our menu celebrates familiar flavours alongside modern café favourites. Every visit helps us support local growers, makers, and the community around us.

Meet the founder

A vision brewed with heart.

Savannah Brew was founded by Amina Otieno, a coffee enthusiast who believes that every cup should tell a story. Her dream was to build a place where people could enjoy excellent Kenyan coffee while feeling genuinely welcome.

Today, that dream lives in every shared table, friendly hello, and carefully poured cup.
